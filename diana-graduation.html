My Dearest Diana,

I don’t have the right words for a moment this big — but I’ll try anyway, because you deserve every word.

3,400 kilometres separate us. But watching you — even from that distance — watching you work, grow, and refuse to give up even on the hardest days… it made me fall in love with you all over again, every single time.
